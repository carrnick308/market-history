About these numbers

  • Data: Robert J. Shiller, U.S. Stock Markets 1871–Present and CAPE Ratio (shillerdata.com). US share market (S&P 500) in US dollars, with dividends reinvested and no fees or tax.
  • P/E is Shiller’s cyclically adjusted P/E (CAPE): price divided by the average of the last 10 years of inflation-adjusted earnings. It needs 10 years of earnings, so it starts in 1881.
  • Returns are the average yearly return of a lump sum invested in that month and held for the look-ahead period. Months from the most recent years aren’t included because their look-ahead period hasn’t finished.
  • A high P/E has been followed by lower returns on average, but it has never told anyone when a fall would come. The P/E first passed its 1929 peak in July 1997, and the market kept rising for more than two years after that.
